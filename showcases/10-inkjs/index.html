          How the night works

          • The ledger tells the night, an hour to an entry. When it stops, decide what the porter does: press 1 to 9, or click or tap a choice.
          • When a guest rings, their window drops its flag on the bell board. The corridor clock keeps the hour. Hollis relieves you at six.
          • Your pocket notebook holds what you have noticed. Consult it whenever you like (N). Things you notice can be said out loud later.
          • Your pockets hold what you carry. What you keep matters, and so does who you help.
          • Nothing is timed but the night itself. Read at your own pace; press Space to let the ink dry faster.
          • The ledger saves itself after every choice. The script view (S) opens the ink the story is written in: change it, rebind the book, and read your own night.
