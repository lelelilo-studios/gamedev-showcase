how the night goes

Six bells till dawn

Twelve herbs grow in the beds. At every bell, things come over the wall to eat them. Lose six and the night is lost. Between bells you choose one charm.

Ember

A seed thrown hot. It bursts into a ring of flame where it lands. Slugs hate it.

Rime

A breath of frost across the ground in front of you. Everything on it slows; snails freeze solid, and a frozen snail shatters at the next touch.

Spark

A chain bolt from the lantern that jumps between up to four foes. It is the only thing that cracks a beetle's shell.

SlugsSoft and slow. Burn them.
SnailsFreeze them with Rime, then break them with anything.
BeetlesSpark the shell, then burn. They charge: watch for the red lane and skip aside.
