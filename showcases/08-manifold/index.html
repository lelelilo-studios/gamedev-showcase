      How to play

      1. Pick a brush from the kit: box, plank, ramp, stairs, arch, cylinder. Each ticket gives you a few.
      2. Set it to ADD to build, or SUB to carve through whatever is there. Doorways, tunnels and windows are just subtractions.
      3. Point at the level and stamp. Aim at a wall's face to stand the brush on the floor; aim at a top to set it on (or dig into) that surface. Every stamp re-runs the boolean, live.
      4. Press P to walk the route as the test walker. Touch the bell rope in the tower to close the ticket.
      5. Spare brushes and a quick walk score more. Six tickets before the 18:00 build; miss it and the build goes out without you.
      The walker is 1.80 m tall, climbs steps up to 45 cm and slopes up to 45°. He never jumps or crouches. Water and the void send him back to the start pad.
