    Rules of the Trial

    1. Outrun. Send your dog wide round the back of the flock. Cut in across their noses and the judge will see it.
    2. Lift & fetch. Walk them steady off the top, down the fell and over the bridge — the only way across the beck — to the handler's post.
    3. Drive. Push them through the gates, between the flags and in the direction of the arrow on the plan.
    4. Pen. Work them into the pen. When every ewe is in, the shepherd shuts the gate and the clock stops.

    Points are taken off, never given: for bolting, splitting, barking, bumping a ewe, missed gates, and five for every sheep lost over the wall. A calm flock and time in hand are rewarded on the card.
