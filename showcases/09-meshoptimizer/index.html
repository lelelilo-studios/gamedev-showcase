How to fly

Stick
AD or ←→ bank. WS or ↑↓ pitch. Or hold the mouse button and drag: wherever you press becomes the centre of the stick.
Speed
Hold Shift to dive and trade height for speed. Hold Space for the air brake when you're too high on approach.
Lift
Circling birds and rising seeds mark a thermal, with a cloud on top. Bank into a steady circle where the vario chirps fastest, and climb.
Hoops
Six of the ranger's hoops mark the route down the valley. Through the middle counts as clean.
Landing
Lantern Meadow is the mown strip at the far end. Arrive low and slow, wings level, and touch down gently near the white T.
Lens
L tints every tree by its level of detail. F freezes the choice, so you can turn back and see what the distance was hiding.
Sun
About four and a half minutes of light. Land before dusk. Esc or P pauses, M mutes.
Phone
Left thumb anywhere on the left is the stick. Right thumb holds Dive or Brake. L and F sit on the triangle panel.
