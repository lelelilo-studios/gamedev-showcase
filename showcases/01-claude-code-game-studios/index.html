    How the night goes

    1. Keep every bay below −10 °C. A cooler that frosts over stops cooling: stand at it and hold Work to scrape it clear.
    2. The generator drinks fuel. Cans are in the shed outside, where the cold gets into you. Bring one down and hold Work to pour.
    3. When it coughs, it is about to stall. Crank it: press Work while the needle is in the green, three times. A miss costs one.
    4. Above −10 °C seeds die by the second. Fall below half the collection and the vault is lost. Make it to 06:00.
