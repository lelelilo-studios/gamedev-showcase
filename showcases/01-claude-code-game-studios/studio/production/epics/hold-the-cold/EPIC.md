## Goal

Work the storm night alone at the Hesper Fell seed vault, running between frosting coolers, a thirsty generator and the blizzard outside, and keep three bays of seeds below the thaw line until the relief crew comes at six.

## Scope (the brief's MVP)

1. The vault in cross-section and a keeper who walks and climbs it.
2. Bay cooling, frost and scraping.
3. The generator, its fuel and the run outside for cans.
4. The storm night: clock, seeded events, stalls and the crank.
5. Losses above the thaw line, win, lose, end screen.
6. Title, how-to-play, pause, settings, HUD.
7. Sound and feedback.

## Order

The brief's build order is the plan (no sprint plan at `minimal`): stories 001 to 007, in number order.
