The written plan from the first playtest, replayed on the tuned build (desktop, seed 20261019):
the collection was down to 2,784 by 03:00 and the vault was lost. Most of that was the tuning
doing its job; two parts were bugs:

- **BUG-0001 (S2)** — a gust blew the keeper off the shed during the pickup; no can, a dry tank, a
