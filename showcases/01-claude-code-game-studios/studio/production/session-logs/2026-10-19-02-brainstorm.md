## Step 1: Concept

**Studio:** Three one-line concepts from your hint. Pick one, or combine them.

1. **Hold the Cold.** Keep three seed bays below the thaw line through a storm night: scrape frosted
   coolers, haul fuel in from the blizzard and crank the generator back to life until the relief
   crew arrives at six. *(plate-spinner × seed vault)*
2. **Shelf Life.** Catch and re-shelve the seed boxes the storm's tremors shake loose, matching labels
   under a failing lamp before the morning audit. *(sorting puzzle × seed vault)*
3. **Radio the Ridge.** Talk the diesel snowcat up the mountain road to the vault by radio, reading
   marker poles through the whiteout before the vault's tanks run dry. *(driving by voice × storm)*

**Human:** One, *Hold the Cold*. The night shift is the game itself: the clock to six is the win
and the thaw line is the loss, so it can be won or lost without anything bolted on. Its riskiest
question (is running between failing machines fun?) is the first thing we can build and test. It
fits on one screen, and the storm still gets in: you step out into it for fuel. *Shelf Life* is a
reading test with the storm as wallpaper, and *Radio the Ridge* takes the player out of the vault,
which is the place the hint is about.
