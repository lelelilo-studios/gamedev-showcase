How to keep a village

  1. Aim the branchPoint the mouse at a roof, or drag a thumb anywhere over the village. The dotted arc shows where the water will land.
  2. Hold to sprayHold the mouse button or Space. On a phone, keep your thumb down on the village.
  3. Keep her pumpingAlternate A D or ← →, or tap PUMP in a steady rhythm. Pressure is reach: the gauge marks what each terrace needs, I, II, III and the Bell.
  4. Mind the vaneFire jumps downwind and climbs uphill. A soaked roof shrugs off sparks for a while, so wet the next roof before the wind gets there.
  5. Save the bellThe bell tower is the last stand. If it falls, the night is over. Hold out until five o'clock.

This pump runs on WebGPU

Bucket Brigade simulates every flame and plume of smoke as a real fluid on your graphics card, using Fire Pro. Fire Pro runs only on WebGPU and has no WebGL path, and this browser does not offer WebGPU.

  • Open it in a recent Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS or ChromeOS, or in Safari with WebGPU switched on.
  • On Linux with an NVIDIA card, start Chrome with --enable-unsafe-webgpu --enable-features=Vulkan --enable-dawn-features=vulkan_enable_f16_on_nvidia.
